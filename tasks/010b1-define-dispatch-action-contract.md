Status: accepted pure foundation after coordinator/reviewer72 tests/129 assertions, strict/eleven pure checks and CLEAR/APPROVE. Parent010 remains incomplete. [ADR](../docs/decisions/durable-dispatch.md), [review](../docs/reviews/dispatch-010b1-review.md).

Own only new runtime/dispatch-contract.ts, dispatch-state.ts and their two focused tests, this task and unique evidence. No schema, DB, authority issuer, worker, connection store, transport, launcher, environment, provider or composition changes. Four source/test files; reuse accepted006/008/009 primitives where coherent.

Implement strict readonly secret-free action identity and canonical encoding exactly as the ADR's fixed tuple. Every field contributes; canonical decode must re-encode exactly. Version1 fixture supports activate/input and paired null connection/version only; credential-bearing/native/tool actions remain unsupported. IDs use the accepted canonical run/attempt/lease UUIDs and bounded actor/installation/policy fields; positive counters obey int32 and fixture deadlines/budgets. Input descriptors bind SHA256 of exact bounded bytes, sequence/count/EOF; a trusted constructor may hash a copied Uint8Array synchronously, but persisted descriptors never contain bytes. Parsing a stored digest proves shape, not actual byte delivery or actor authority. Activation/input sequence spaces remain separate as specified.

Pure grant state transitions must retain immutable binding, current revision/CAS and pending/issued/cancelled/expired/uncertain plus separate not_attempted/acknowledged/unknown delivery. Issued means admitted once; no route returns to pending and no second effect is permitted by replay, timeout or restart. Unknown commit/send remains non-reissuable. Expiry cannot turn issued/unknown delivery into proof of no effect. Matching facts are inert; changed identity/payload/revision conflicts. No hidden raw-secret/error or external callback. Drafts are not executable authority: future010c/d persists and validates them before any I/O.

Expose a bounded pure capacity projection over validated claims: every unreleased claim counts globally and per owner, including expired, logically terminal, unknown prepare/start and stopping resources. Released claims require the closed logical-terminal plus authoritative-cleanup/no-pending facts; parsing fields does not establish their real provenance. Missing/invalid policy, malformed/duplicate claim identity, counter exhaustion or inconsistent facts fail closed. Fixed fixture limits2 globally/1 per actor; no drifting counter, scheduling or lease replacement.

Do not implement ExecutionAuthority in this pure slice. The accepted design requires a real transaction-backed issuer with locked-row provenance and dead-transaction invalidation; a structural row or injected fake ORM cannot prove that.010b2/c will own that separately after concrete schema/service review. Unit tests here prove exact tuple/all-field substitutions/UTF8 and size bounds, immutable copies, changed replay, CAS, expiry/uncertainty, capacity at limits and terminal-but-unconfirmed occupancy. Include independent expected tuple oracles and failing-first regressions. Focused Bun tests, strict TypeScript/LSP, bounded native pure driver if useful; no fake DB/native completion. Freeze for independent review before dependent storage.

## Source completion and boundary

Four new source/test files implement the exact17-position action tuple and closed activation/input descriptors. Run/attempt/lease/action/receipt identifiers use canonical UUIDs; actor/installation/policy fields reuse bounded run IDs. Fixture credentials are paired null. Input construction synchronously hashes a copied bounded byte array; storage contains only digest/count/sequence/EOF. Historical decoding requires byte-exact canonical re-encoding. Descriptor shape cannot prove real delivered bytes, actual per-attempt budget remaining or actor authority.

Grant construction checks fresh expiry within30seconds and the absolute fixture deadline within60seconds. Stored grants retain canonical binding, revision, timestamps, state and separate delivery outcome. Transitions require exact grant/canonical-action/revision CAS, preserve immutable binding, admit once, and keep issued/uncertain/cancelled/expired replays inert. Cancellation after issuance records intent while retaining spent admission; expiry cannot erase uncertain delivery. Matching acknowledgements preserve the retained revision/timestamps. These are immutable pure drafts with newAdmission metadata, never executable authority or committed commands. Unique action/sequence and semantic-action anti-reminting across rows require the future real storage boundary.

Unknown commit may retain issuedAtMs=null because the issue timestamp was not established. A later exact trusted acknowledgement can set deliveryOutcome=acknowledged while state remains uncertain and issuedAtMs remains null; it invents no issue time or redelivery permission. Stale-revision replay fails CAS and requires rereading current status, never minting another action.

Capacity projection validates at most1024 complete claim facts and fixed policy2/1, rejects duplicate attempt/claim-operation identities and inconsistent release facts, then counts every unreleased claim. Logical termination and lease expiry do not reduce occupancy. Release requires terminal logical state, confirmed cleanup, no pending operation and valid chronology. This validates structural consistency only; later DB/launcher integration supplies provenance and the authoritative full snapshot. The maximum bound is a fail-closed projection limit, never silent truncation.
