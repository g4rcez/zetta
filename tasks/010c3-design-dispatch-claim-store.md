# RFC-010c3: Design transaction-backed fixture claims

Status: revised design accepted after [coordinator independent review](../docs/reviews/dispatch-claim-design-010c3-review.md),2026-10-22. Parent010 remains incomplete. Design-only ownership: [dispatch claim store decision](../docs/decisions/dispatch-claim-store.md), this task and unique evidence. Pure codec/row implementation requires a separate assignment; no schema/DB/VM/container/environment/provider/Git changes.

Inputs: accepted010a/010b1/010c1/010c2/010c2a and actual005/007/009/008 source. The proposal defines explicit policy bootstrap, trusted durable fixture producer provenance and activation revisions, transaction-local reserve-purpose ExecutionAuthority, exact stored codecs, ordered atomic lease+claim insertion, complete occupancy aggregate and same-operation repair. No automatic prepare, grant, worker, transport or release of possibly submitted resources.

Critical inspected limits:009 has no real bot/conversation ownership queries or durable producer activation revision; parsed rows alone cannot mint backend authority. Its running owner-cancel records intent but cannot no-effect-terminalize a leased never-submitted attempt. A separately reviewed009 cancelled_before_admission extension is required for atomic no-effect owner cancellation/release; until accepted, cancellation retains occupancy and never fabricates completion/stop. New factory/replay is distinguished from true process restart. Native credentials, background enrollment continuity, backend reasons and real launcher-fact provenance remain explicit integration gates.

The selected durable prerequisite adds a005 monotonic positive-int32 activationRevision and immutable009 producer kind/version/actorActivationRevision, stamped solely by the trusted submission transaction. Legacy all-null provenance remains ineligible and request replay never rewrites it. There is no transient ticket protocol. Private backend reservation derives its owner from locked stored provenance; owner get/cancel require issued actors. ExecutionAuthority is privately issued/verified inside the concrete transaction and invalidated in finally, never returned or serialized. Separate005/009 migration/integration, codec, store and actual proof children stay bounded to at most five files each.

No implementation test or live scenario was run; future actual proof cases are a plan, not results.
